Sermon Processor

Sermon Processor is River Lake Church's tool for turning the recording of a Sunday service into a finished sermon video and podcast episode. Church media volunteers use it each week.

Open the app For River Lake Church volunteers. Sign-in required.

What it does

  1. Picks up the recording. A volunteer puts the service recording in the church's Google Drive folder.
  2. Edits it. In the app, the volunteer marks where the sermon starts and ends, can keep several parts, and can add the church's end graphic. The edited video and an MP3 for the podcast are saved back to the church's Drive.
  3. Writes a summary. A short title and description of the sermon are drafted with Google Gemini, for the volunteer to review.
  4. Prepares YouTube. If the volunteer asks, the edited video is uploaded to the church's own YouTube channel as a private video, with its title, description and thumbnail. Nothing goes public until a person publishes it in YouTube Studio.
  5. Publishes the podcast. A volunteer reviews the episode and adds it to the church's podcast feed, which podcast apps such as Spotify and Apple Podcasts read.

How it uses Google accounts

Sermon Processor connects only to the church's own Google account and YouTube channel, which a church administrator signs in once to approve:

  • Google Drive: to read the service recordings, graphics and thumbnails in the church's Sermon Processor folder, and to save the edited videos, podcast audio and descriptions back to that folder.
  • YouTube: to upload edited videos to the church's channel as private videos and set their title, description and thumbnail.

The data is used only to do this work for the church. It is never sold, used for advertising, or shared beyond the services listed in the Privacy Policy.

Who runs it

Sermon Processor is run by volunteers at River Lake Church for the church's own use. It is not offered to the public. Questions: boysee4545@gmail.com.

The church podcast's public feed is at /podcast/feed.xml.
